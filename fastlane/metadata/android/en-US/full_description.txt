A live world map of churches, coloured by what is happening inside them right now.

Every dot is a real church building, mapped from open data. Green means it is meeting now, blue means
it already met earlier today, red means it is not meeting yet. Tap any dot for the church, its
tradition, its local time and the next service.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT
Most church finders are lists. Church Radar is a radar: one glance tells you what is happening in a
city, a country or the whole world at this moment. The colours come from published service times and
from typical hours per tradition when a church publishes none — the app always tells you which one it
used, and shows a "?" when it is only an estimate.

56,946 CHURCHES, EVERY CONTINENT
Zoom out for the globe and the dots thin out so the world stays readable; zoom into a city and every
church appears. Past street level the app scans OpenStreetMap live for the churches around you,
including the small ones.

COMMUNITY CHECK-INS, IN REAL TIME
If you are at a service that is not on the schedule, tap "It's meeting now". Every open radar updates
in under a tenth of a second — no reload, no account, no sign-in. Someone else can confirm, and the
report expires on its own.

MORE THAN ONE MAP
Switch from status to denomination to see Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Protestant and Baptist
churches at a glance, or filter to a single tradition.

MADE TO BE RESPECTFUL
No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Check-ins are one tap of trust and
are presented as community reports, never as verified facts.

OPEN DATA
Church locations from Wikidata and OpenStreetMap, map tiles from OpenStreetMap contributors. Source
code, data scripts and build instructions are on GitHub:
https://github.com/CrossbowCraft13/ChurchRadar

The app needs an internet connection: it loads live churches, map tiles and community check-ins.
